Research into the makespan scheduling problem viewed through the lens of computational argumentation, conducted under the Undergraduate Research Opportunities Programme (UROP) at Imperial College London.

The project explores how argumentation frameworks can model and reason about scheduling conflicts, drawing connections between formal argumentation theory and classical combinatorial optimisation.
